A screening test game

Screening Showdown

Every screening test has a cutoff. Move it one way and you catch more disease, but you also alarm more healthy people. Move it the other way and the reverse happens.

Across five rounds you will tune a test for different settings, from a community screening day to a hunt for a rare disease. Your job is to meet each clinic's goal.

    Cutoffs are trade-offs. Raising sensitivity almost always costs specificity. The right balance depends on what a miss costs versus what a false alarm costs.
    Sensitivity and specificity belong to the test. They stay the same when you move it to a new population.
    Predictive values belong to the population. The same test produces mostly false positives when the disease is rare, and mostly true positives in a high risk clinic.
