About

Research with a pulse.

A public health professional, educator, and strategist working at the intersection of evidence and equity.

Data as a tool for empowerment, and imagination backed by real-world evidence.

Who I Am

I'm a public health professional, educator, and strategist dedicated to advancing equity, improving population health, and supporting organizations working for social good. I combine evidence-based methods with creative communication to help partners make real-world impact.

My Approach

I center community voices, prioritize equity, and approach every project with curiosity and care. My work is grounded in collaborative partnerships, using data as a tool for empowerment, and imaginative thinking backed by real-world evidence.

Services

What I do.

Comprehensive public health consulting services rooted in evidence and driven by impact.

Public Health Strategy & Evaluation

Plan, implement, and measure programs that improve health outcomes and advance equity. From logic models to impact assessments, I bring clarity to complexity.

Curriculum & Educational Design

Design engaging, accessible training and learning experiences for professionals and adult learners. From e-learning to workshops, I create content that resonates.

Facilitation & Strategic Planning

From board retreats to community visioning sessions, I help teams move forward with purpose. Inclusive facilitation creates space for real conversations and actionable plans.

Multimedia & Creative Communications

Craft videos, infographics, toolkits, and more to communicate complex ideas clearly and compellingly. Visual storytelling that brings your work to life.

Grant Writing & Funding Development

Develop compelling grant proposals and funding strategies to secure resources for public health programs. From federal grants to foundation funding, I help organizations translate their vision into fundable projects that drive community impact.

Participatory Action Research & Evaluation

Partner with communities to co-create research and evaluation projects that center lived experience and drive meaningful change. Using participatory methods, I help organizations engage stakeholders as partners in knowledge generation and program improvement.
