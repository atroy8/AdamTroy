Annual view

The split is clearer year by year

Firearm homicide rose abruptly in 2020, peaked in 2021, then declined. Firearm suicide changed less abruptly and reached its highest annual count in this series in 2024.

Definitions and limits

What these lines measure

Death-certificate counts by month of death for U.S. residents. The chart covers all 84 months with no missing or suppressed values.

Intent categories

  • Suicide: ICD-10 X72–X74, intentional self-harm by firearm discharge.
  • Homicide: ICD-10 X93–X95, assault by firearm discharge.
  • The homicide series excludes ICD-10 U01.4, terrorism involving firearm discharge. That excludes 17 deaths in August 2019 and none in the other months shown.

Data status and rates

  • 2018–2020 are final CDC WONDER D76 data. 2021–2024 are provisional D176 data and may be revised as late certificates are processed.
  • Rates are crude monthly deaths per 100,000 residents, not age-adjusted or annualized. Each month uses that year’s annual population denominator.
  • Coding practices and reporting timeliness vary by jurisdiction.

Source: CDC WONDER, National Vital Statistics System. Final Underlying Cause of Death data for 2018–2020 and Provisional Multiple Cause of Death data for 2021–2024. Pandemic markers are identified by the cited agencies and reporting organizations shown on each card.
